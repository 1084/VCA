Version 2026-10-30 · Effective November 1, 2026

Cove Rooms is a small service run by John Hubert LLC, a Virginia company. These terms are written to be read. If anything here is unclear, write to support@johnhubert.llc and a person will answer.

1. Who these terms are for

There are two kinds of people in a Cove room. A host rents a room, holds its link, and decides who comes in. A guest is anyone the host lets in. Hosts have accounts and agree to these terms when they open a room. Guests have no account; by entering a room they agree to the conduct rule and to the parts of these terms that speak to guests (sections 5, 6 and 9).

Hosts must be at least 18. Guests may be any age the host is responsible for admitting; see section 6.

2. What you are renting

A room is a private video room for up to ten people with a name, a link, a lockable door, shared notes, and, for Studio rooms, a front-door page and a wall that keeps videos, files, homework and messages between sessions. The room is yours to use for the time you have paid for. It is not listed anywhere; you give the link to whom you choose.

Rooms are offered by invitation. We may decline an application or withdraw an invitation, and we may change what a room includes as the service develops. If a change removes something you rely on, you may cancel and we will refund any unused time.

3. Rent and payment

Two things are for sale: a monthly lease that renews until you cancel, and a day pass that covers 24 hours and simply expires. Current prices are shown on the rooms page and again on the payment page before you pay. Prices may change for future periods; a change never applies to a period you have already paid for.

Payments are taken by Stripe on Stripe's own pages. We never see or store your card number. Your lease can be changed or cancelled at any time from the Billing link in your room list; cancelling stops the next charge, and the room stays open until the end of the period already paid.

If a renewal payment fails, the room stays open for three days while Stripe retries. If it still fails, the door closes until rent is paid. Nothing is deleted at that moment; see section 8.

Some rooms are provided without charge to invited hosts. We may end a free arrangement with thirty days' notice.

4. Refunds

Our refund policy is short:

  • If the service was unavailable for a meaningful part of a period you paid for, tell us and we will refund that period, in full for a day pass and pro rata for a month.
  • If you cancel a monthly lease within seven days of its first charge and have not held a session in the room, we refund that charge in full on request.
  • Otherwise a monthly lease runs to the end of the paid period and is not refunded for the unused part, and a day pass is not refunded once it has started.
  • If we close your room under section 7 for a breach of the conduct rule, no refund is owed. If we close it for any other reason, we refund the unused time.

Ask by email. Refunds go back to the card that paid, through Stripe, usually within ten business days.

5. What is not for sale

Cove never handles the money between a host and their students or guests. Any lesson fee, tip or package is arranged and paid directly between you, through whatever links the host chooses to show. We take no commission, hold no funds, and are not a party to those arrangements. A dispute about a lesson is between host and guest; we will help where a report shows the conduct rule was broken, but we do not adjudicate payment disputes.

6. Hosts are responsible for their rooms

You decide who enters your room, and you are responsible for what happens in it. In particular:

  • You confirm you have the right to teach, meet or work with the people you admit, including any permission a parent or school requires when a guest is a minor.
  • You keep your sign-in to yourself. If you think someone else has it, change your password and tell us.
  • Anything you pin to a wall is yours or you have the right to share it. We may remove material we are told infringes someone's rights, and we respond to notices sent to support@johnhubert.llc.
  • You use the room in a way that keeps with the conduct rule, and you remove or report anyone who does not.

7. When we step in

We cannot see or hear inside rooms. We act on reports, which any participant can send with the Report button, and on what we can observe: who holds a room and when people join and leave. If a report shows the conduct rule was broken, we may remove a participant, lock or close a room, or end a host's account. For anything involving a child's safety we act first and ask questions after, and we cooperate with law enforcement. We will tell a host why their room was closed unless doing so would put someone at risk.

8. Data, notes and what happens when you leave

What we store, and for how long, is set out in the privacy policy. In short: when a room closes because rent lapses or you delete it, its notes stay exportable for thirty days and are then deleted, and its wall and files are deleted with it. Rooms with no host session for ninety days are archived and then deleted the same way, after we write to you.

9. What we promise, and what we do not

We run the service with care, keep it small enough to answer a report in minutes, and tell you plainly when something breaks. We do not promise that it will never be unavailable; see section 4 for what happens when it is. The service is provided as it is, and to the extent the law allows, our liability to you for anything arising from it is limited to the rent you paid us in the twelve months before the claim. We are not liable for what participants say or do in a room, for material a host pins, or for the arrangements between a host and their guests.

10. Changing these terms

When we change these terms we post the new version here with a new date and, for anything material, email hosts at least fourteen days before it takes effect. Continuing to use your room after that date is acceptance. If you do not accept a change, cancel before it takes effect and we refund any unused time.

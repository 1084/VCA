Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-30 · Effective November 1, 2026

Cove is built so that we see as little as possible. This page lists exactly what we store, for how long, and which other companies touch it. If a sentence here ever stops being true, we change the product or we change the sentence.

What we never see

Video and audio travel directly between the people in a room, encrypted end to end. There is no platform recording and no server in the middle that can watch or listen. When a direct path fails (about one call in six, behind strict networks), the encrypted stream is relayed through a Cloudflare TURN server that forwards the bytes without being able to read them.

We do not read chat, and chat is not stored: it exists in the browsers of the people in the room and vanishes when they leave.

Guests: nothing

Guests have no account. To enter a room a guest types a name; that name is held in the browser for the session and shown to others in the room. We do not store it. We keep no record of a guest beyond the join and leave times described below, which carry no name and no address.

Captions and translation

Live captions use the speech recognition built into your browser. On many devices that recognition runs on the device; on some, the browser's maker (Apple or Google) processes the audio under their own policies. Cove's servers never receive audio. In Language rooms, the text of a caption is sent to DeepL for translation when the host has turned translation on; no name or identifier goes with it, and nothing is kept.

Walls and files

Files pinned to a Studio wall are uploaded straight from the browser to storage we rent from Cloudflare (R2), in a private bucket. They are reached only through links that expire after ten minutes and are issued only to people holding a key to that room. Hand-ins are visible to the host and to the student who sent them, and to no one else. Taking a pin down deletes the file.

Payments

Rent is paid on Stripe's pages. Stripe receives your card details and billing address and is the controller of that data under its own privacy policy. We receive confirmation of payment and the ids needed to match it to your room. We never see a card number.

Children

Guests have no accounts and we collect nothing from them, which is how we approach children's privacy: there is nothing to collect. Hosts who teach children are responsible for having the parent's knowledge and any permission their situation requires, as set out in the conduct rule. Parents who want to know what a room holds about their child may write to us; the answer will almost always be "nothing", and we will show you how we know.

Your rights

Write to support@johnhubert.llc to see, export, correct or delete anything we hold about you. Hosts can export notes and delete their room themselves at any time. We answer within a week and complete deletions within thirty days, except for the payment records the law requires us to keep. You may complain to your data protection authority; we would rather you wrote to us first.

Security

Connections use TLS. Passwords are hashed. Keys to rooms are short-lived and scoped to one room. Admin access requires a key held by one person. If we ever discover a breach that affects you, we will tell you within 72 hours of knowing, in plain words.

Changes

We post changes here with a new version date and email hosts about anything material at least fourteen days before it takes effect.

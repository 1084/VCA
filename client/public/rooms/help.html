When something isn't working

Troubleshooting · updated October 31, 2026

Cove runs in the browser with nothing to install, so most problems are one setting away. Start with the first section; it fixes the majority of reports we get from iPhones.

iPhone or iPad: you can't see or hear the others, or captions say the browser can't transcribe

Check Lockdown Mode first. Settings → Privacy & Security → Lockdown Mode. If it's on, Safari blocks the parts of the web that live video and on-device captions need. Turn it off for the lesson (or add cove.johnhubert.llc as an excluded site: in Safari tap aA in the address bar → Website Settings → turn off Lockdown Mode for this site), then reload the room.

You'll know Lockdown Mode is the cause if Safari shows Lockdown Enabled under the address bar, or the captions band reads "This browser can't transcribe."

The door says "Wait at the door" or nothing happens when you knock

The host hasn't opened the room yet, or they're not in it. Knocks only reach a host who is inside. Stay on the page; when they arrive, your knock goes through and they tap Let in.

If you were already in the room and find yourself at the door again, your connection dropped for a moment (locking the phone or switching apps does it). You're let back in automatically as long as the host is still there.

People can't hear you

  • Check the mic button at the bottom: red means muted.
  • iPhone: Settings → Safari → Microphone → Allow, then reload. In the room, the first tap on Mic asks permission; if you declined once, Safari remembers until you change it there.
  • Bluetooth earbuds sometimes grab the mic but not the speaker, or the other way round. Try with them off once to tell which.
  • Another app may hold the microphone (a call, a voice memo). End it and tap Mic again.

You can't hear them

Browsers won't play sound until you've touched the page. If you see Tap to enable incoming audio, tap it. Check the ring/silent switch on an iPhone: Safari respects it for web audio on some iOS versions. Check the volume.

Video is black or frozen

  • Camera permission: iPhone Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow. Android: the lock icon in the address bar → Permissions.
  • Only one app can use the camera at a time. Close any other video app.
  • On a weak connection Cove keeps the audio and lets the video stall. Move closer to the router or off a VPN; corporate VPNs often block the relay that video needs.
  • Still stuck: leave and rejoin. A fresh connection fixes most one-off freezes.

Captions

  • Captions are made by each person's own phone and shared as text. You caption your voice; you read theirs. If yours don't appear, unmute first.
  • iPhone: captions use Dictation. Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation. And see Lockdown Mode above.
  • Firefox and some privacy browsers have no speech recognition at all; you'll still see everyone else's captions.
  • Pick the language you'll speak with the EN button; captioning the wrong language produces nonsense.

The Wall

  • The Wall is only in Studio rooms, and only once you're inside; it isn't on the front door.
  • Uploads: videos up to 100 MB (MP4, WebM or MOV), files up to 25 MB. A file that fails instantly is usually a type we don't accept (we list the common ones) or a size over the limit.
  • Hand-ins are seen by the host and the student who sent them, nobody else. That's on purpose.

Links and the room code

A named room's link looks like cove.johnhubert.llc/r/your-room. Open it in the browser, not inside the Messages or Instagram preview, which sometimes blocks the camera; tap the link, then if it opened in an in-app browser, use its menu to open in Safari or Chrome.

Hosts: rent, the door, and removing someone

  • Rent status and the Rent / Billing buttons are in the lobby under My rooms. Payment happens on Stripe's page; if you come back and the status hasn't changed after a minute, reload — and if it shows "Rent status: … Retry", your sign-in has likely expired; sign in again.
  • Door locks and unlocks. Locked means guests knock and you let them in; unlocked means anyone with the link walks in.
  • Remove (in People) puts someone out, keeps them out for the rest of your session, and locks the door.

Still stuck?

Write to support@johnhubert.llc with the room name, the device and browser, and what you saw. A person reads it, usually the same day. Please don't send recordings; we don't need them and we'd rather not have them.

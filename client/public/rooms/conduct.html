The conduct rule

Version 2026-10-30

Every host agrees to this before opening a room, and every guest agrees to it at the door. It is short on purpose.

Treat the room like a classroom with the door open. Everyone in it is there for the lesson or the meeting, and nothing happens in it that you would not do with a parent, a colleague or a stranger watching from the hall.

What that means in practice

  • Be who you say you are. Use your real name or the one your students know you by. Do not enter a room as someone else or let someone else use your key.
  • Keep it about the work. A room is for teaching, learning, meeting and studying. No sexual content or conduct of any kind, no harassment, no threats, no slurs, no hate.
  • Children are off limits for anything but the lesson. No private contact with a minor outside what the lesson needs and the parent knows about. No asking a child for photos, personal details or secrecy. No one-on-one time that a parent has not arranged. Parents may join any room at any time, and a host may never refuse them.
  • Do not record people without their knowledge. Cove does not record. If you record a session yourself, say so first, and never share it beyond the people who were in it.
  • Pin only what you may share. Videos, files and messages on a wall are yours, or you have permission for them. No pirated material, no malware, nothing that violates anyone's privacy.
  • Money stays honest. Lesson fees are between host and student. No pressure, no surprise charges, no "pay here" links that go anywhere other than where they say.
  • Nothing illegal. If it is a crime where you are, it is a breach here.

If something happens

Every participant has a Report button. It sends us the room, the time and your words, never any video or audio. A person reads it. We can remove someone, lock a room, close it or end a host's account within minutes, and we do. For anything involving a child's safety we act first and may involve law enforcement.

Hosts can also act on the spot: Remove takes someone out of the room and keeps them out for the session, and locks the door.

If you are a parent and something about a lesson worries you, write to support@johnhubert.llc. You will reach a person, not a form.

What breaking it costs

A host who breaks this rule loses their room without a refund. A guest who breaks it is removed and kept out. Where a law is broken we report it. There is no appeal process to speak of because there is nothing complicated here: the rule is the one above, and we apply it.
